
SRP8-124 

Hydrogen Energy System 

The Hydrogen Control System directly interfaces with SRP8-124 to receive real-time sensor data from the hydrogen energy system, including pressure, temperature, and flow measurements on both the anode and cathode sides of the fuel cell. SRP8-186 also sends control signals to actuators such as mass-flow controllers, purge valves, and back-pressure regulators. This interface enables closed-loop control of hydrogen and air pressure and flow, safe hydrogen handling, and coordinated startup and shutdown procedures. 

SRP8-182 

Hydrogen Power Electronics System 

SRP8-186 interfaces with SRP8-182 to manage current draw from the fuel cell through the DC-DC converter. During startup, the control system ensures current draw is enabled only after stable operating pressures are achieved. During steady-state operation, feedback is used to regulate power output. In fault or abnormal conditions, SRP8-186 commands SRP8-182 to reduce or disable current draw as part of a protective shutdown sequence.
