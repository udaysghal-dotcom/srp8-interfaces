SRP8-093 

Air Conditioning 

Prelim Air Conditioning document says vent routing is up to interior. 

The recirculation inlet and all the outlet vents are integrated into the front of the dashboard (close to the driver) for interior air conditioning. Larger components such as the blower, evaporator and condenser will be supported by a shelf on the inside of the dashboard structure/skeleton according to their vent configurations.  

Additionally, vent sizes and specifications need to be confirmed to reserve spaces for holes into the dashboard to allow the airvents to function.
