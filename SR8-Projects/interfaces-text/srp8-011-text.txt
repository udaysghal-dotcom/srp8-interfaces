2.3.1 Project/System Interfaces 

Assembly 

Requirement 

SRP8-014: Regen Braking 

The central stop lamp must be illuminated when a certain magnitude of deceleration is produced by the regenerative braking 

SRP8-017: Auxiliary Power Distribution 

The central stop lamp will be powered by the 12V power critical bus. 

SRP8-026: Pedals 

The central stop lamp must be illuminated when the driver suppresses the brake pedal 

SRP8-099: Traction Control 

The central stop lamp must be illuminated if the traction control system deems necessary to apply braking force 


2.3.2 Mounting Points / Areas / Routing 

Assembly 

Requirement 

SRP8-006: Exterior Surfaces 

The central stop lamp must be designed to integrate smoothly with the car’s exterior surface 

SRP8-011: Taillights 

The central stop lamp must illuminate in conjunction with the brake lights within the taillights 

SRP-124: Hydrogen Energy System 

With the hydrogen system placed right below the central stop lamp, it must be ensured that there is no interference with the lamp’s wiring or function.
