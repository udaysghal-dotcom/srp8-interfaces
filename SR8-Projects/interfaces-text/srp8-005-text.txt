2.3.1 Project/System Interfaces 

Project interfaces are with SRP8-065 Occupant Cell for mounting of the hinge .  

The Bonnet Solar Panel (SRP8-020) that is bonded to the bonnet where there needs to be a bond surface, holes for the junction box and the solar sensor. These are also seen in 2.1.1 Normal Operation.  

There needs to be space in the front support structure (SRP8-067 Front Clam Shell, SRP8-066 Front Crash Structures) to mount the physical latch where the striker is on the bonnet, which will be directly under the striker.  

Sealing to the front support structure, this is again not a problem as there is a large surface around the bonnet that allows for this to occur.
